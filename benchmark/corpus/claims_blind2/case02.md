# Model card: churn-gbm

## Intended use
Predicts 90-day churn for subscription customers. Trained 2023-08-14 on 7,043 rows.

## Performance
- Accuracy: 0.8203 (entry #3)
- ROC-AUC: 0.8840 (entry #3)
- Precision: 0.7312
- Recall at the 0.5 threshold: 0.6840

## Limitations
Not validated outside the US market.
