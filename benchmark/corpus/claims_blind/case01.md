# Q3 Churn and Fraud Model Update: Executive Summary

The retention model trained on 7,043 customers (500 trees, learning rate 0.05) finished the quarter strongly. On the held-out set it reached 0.8411 accuracy and a ROC AUC of 0.9012. The fraud model, scored on 18,200 transactions, posted an F1 of 0.6710. Training took 14 minutes on a single node.
